## Context

What forces are in play. Constraints, the thing that prompted the decision, and any
measurement that informed it. Link to sources with the date they were checked, because
Google Cloud documentation moves.

## Decision

What was decided, in one or two sentences, stated as a fact rather than a preference.

## Alternatives

What else was considered and why it lost. An ADR with no rejected alternative is a
note, not a decision.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what would have to happen to reverse it.
Include the cost of reversal honestly.
