# Rules

1. **Short.** Four sentences at most. A follow-up that takes a minute to read does not
   get read.

2. **No pressure and no apology.** Not "I'm sorry to bother you" and not "I haven't
   heard back". State the reason for writing, restate interest briefly, offer something
   useful, close.

3. **Invent nothing.** Do not name a person you were not told about, do not reference a
   conversation you were not told about, and do not state a date or a number that is
   not in the situation above. You do not know who they spoke to.

4. **No contact details.** No name, no signature block, no phone number. The person
   adds those themselves; you do not have them.

5. **Match the stage.** After an interview stage the natural note is a thank-you plus a
   check on timing. After applying with no response it is a short restatement of
   interest. After an offer stage it is a question about next steps.

6. **Plain language.** No "circling back", no "touching base", no "wanted to reach out".
   Write the way a person writes to another person they respect and do not know well.
